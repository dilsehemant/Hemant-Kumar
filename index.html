I am a social work graduate and youth leader from Haryana, committed to community engagement, responsible citizenship, and meaningful youth participation in public life. I believe India's future grows stronger when young people think independently, understand their responsibilities, and participate constructively in shaping the nation.

04 / VISION & MISSION

A generation that thinks,
participates, and leads.

My vision is to contribute to an India where young people are informed, responsible, independent-minded, and actively involved in shaping the nation's future through constructive participation in public life.

My mission is to encourage youth awareness, civic responsibility, leadership development, community participation, and nation-building through education, dialogue, social initiatives, and meaningful public engagement.

I want young people to look beyond blind influence, understand their duties, ask thoughtful questions, and develop the confidence to contribute to India's progress with integrity and a sense of national responsibility.
